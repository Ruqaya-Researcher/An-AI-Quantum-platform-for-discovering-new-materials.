What are the main problems with current batteries?

Current batteries have several major limitations. They charge slowly, heat up during use, and their energy is depleted relatively quickly.


Why can't we simply build perfect batteries?

Because developing better batteries requires discovering or designing new materials. This process is expensive, time-consuming, and classical computer simulations become inefficient for many complex materials..


How could our project help?

Our goal is to accelerate the discovery of new battery materials by using quantum computing to simulate their properties more efficiently and accurately than traditional approaches where possible.
